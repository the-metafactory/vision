# metafactory — vision & principles

The public home of the metafactory vision. The build happens across
[the-metafactory](https://github.com/the-metafactory) repos and the
community Discord; this repo holds the *why* — readable by anyone,
without access to the internal planning repos.

## Contents

- **[VISION.md](VISION.md)** — the mission, the insight, what we
  distribute, why trust is foundational, the three layers, and the
  six principles.
- **[factory-of-factories.md](factory-of-factories.md)** — the
  meta-model under metafactory: what "the factory of factories"
  commits us to, the tower it stands on, and the test the meta layer
  must pass.

## Discussing

Pushback and questions are welcome — open an issue here, or bring it
to the community Discord. These are living documents: they change by
discussion, and the history of that change stays visible.
